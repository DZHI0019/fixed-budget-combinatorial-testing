# Transfer evaluation protocol

Use Ferreira et al.'s eight named SPLC 2020 Dataset A systems. Include every system whose public configurations can be matched to the published census, with at least 12 distinct configurations; report exclusions and mismatches. Do not tune parameters on outcome labels. Read enabled-feature sets only in selection. A feature that never varies is removed. Give each realizable assignment on a pair of distinct binary features one point.

Use three fixed seeds 261126, 261127 and 261128, 100 restarts each, as in the prior JHipster experiment. Choose by score only, then lower seed; order candidates by their canonical feature tuples. Each plan must contain exactly 12 distinct configurations. Break zero-gain ties among unselected configurations only. Compare a single-start greedy plan and uniform sampling without replacement at the same budget. Report expected random pair coverage and expected number of selected historically failing configurations via their exact finite-population formulas. Also report probability of hitting at least one failed configuration under uniform sampling. Do not infer root-cause counts from failure events or stack traces.

Freeze the selected plans including hashes before joining outcome labels. Record projects affected by disagreements between the paper counts, archive counts, and failing-configuration records. Published aggregate failure totals were known in advance; this is retrospective external validation, not time-forward deployment. Do not extrapolate the selected fraction of failed configurations to bug detection in unrelated projects.
